My Thoughts

The Things That Live Inside My Mind

There are some feelings that are difficult to explain. Some thoughts are never spoken out loud. So I decided to put a few of them here.

If My Mind Could Speak...

It would probably talk about the little things. The conversations. The smiles. The waiting. The memories. The moments that seemed ordinary at the time but somehow became important later. Sometimes I wonder how one person can become such a big part of someone's thoughts. And somehow... I already know the answer.

A Thought For You

Insiyah, I don't know where life takes us from here. I don't know what tomorrow looks like, and I don't know which memories will stay with us forever. But I do know that the time we shared became something meaningful to me. There are moments I will remember, lessons I will carry, and feelings that helped shape the person I am becoming. Maybe that's what some people are meant to do in our lives. They leave behind memories, lessons and a part of themselves.
