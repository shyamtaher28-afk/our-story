Our Promises

Words We Once Gave To Each Other

Promises are more than words. They are little pieces of hope, memories of what we believed, and reminders of what once mattered. These are some promises I want to keep close to my heart.

A Promise Is A Memory Too ❤️

Sometimes we remember conversations. Sometimes we remember places. And sometimes we remember the things we promised. Whether life keeps those promises exactly the way we imagined or not, the meaning behind them can still remain special. So I wrote mine here.

The Promises

Eight little promises from my heart.

01
❤️

I Promise To Remember

I promise that I will remember the beautiful moments. The conversations, the laughter, the smiles, and the little things that made ordinary days special.

02
🫶

I Promise To Appreciate

I promise to appreciate what we had instead of only thinking about what we didn't have. Every meaningful moment deserves to be remembered with gratitude.

03
🌙

I Promise To Be Kind

Even when things become difficult, I promise to remember that kindness matters. Some words can stay in someone's heart for a very long time.

04
🌱

I Promise To Grow

I promise to keep becoming a better version of myself. To learn from mistakes, understand more, and carry the lessons our story taught me.

05
🎶

I Promise To Remember The Little Things

Sometimes the smallest things become the strongest memories. A song. A laugh. A conversation. A simple moment. I promise not to forget how meaningful little things can be.

06
🌧️

I Promise To Respect Your Journey

Everyone has their own path. I promise to respect yours, even when it takes you somewhere different from where I imagined. Caring about someone also means respecting their choices.

07
✨

I Promise To Keep The Good Memories

Whatever changes with time, I promise to keep the beautiful memories as beautiful memories. Not every chapter needs to remain open for the chapter to have mattered.

08
♾️

I Promise To Never Forget The Impact

Some people change our lives without even realizing it. I promise I will remember the person I became, the things I learned, and the memories I carry because of this chapter.

💌

The Promise That Matters Most

Insiyah, if I could keep only one promise, it would be this: I promise to remember our story with honesty. Not only the perfect moments, but the difficult ones too. Not only the happiness, but everything that made the journey real. Because whatever happens in life, I never want to pretend that something meaningful never happened.

— Taher ❤️

A Page From My Heart 📖

Dear Insiyah...

I don't know what the future has written for us. Maybe life will surprise us. Maybe we will look back at these memories years from now. But if there is one thing I know, it is that the moments we shared became a part of my life. So this is my promise: I will keep learning. I will keep growing. I will keep becoming someone I can be proud of. And I will always be grateful for the chapter that taught me so much about love, memories and myself.

— Taher

One Promise. One Memory. One Heart.

Some promises are spoken. Some are written. And some are simply kept quietly inside the heart. This one is for the memories that will always have a place here.

I promise to keep moving forward, while carrying the beautiful memories with me.

And wherever life takes me, I will always be thankful that our paths crossed.

Some promises are forever in the memories they create.
