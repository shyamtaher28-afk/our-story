One Last Letter

To Insiyah ❤️

Dear Insiyah,

If you ever find yourself looking through these pages, I hope you understand why I created them.

It wasn't just to remember a person. It was to remember a chapter of my life.

The conversations, the smiles, the songs, the silly moments, the difficult days, and all those tiny things that probably seemed ordinary at the time.

Somehow, those ordinary moments became some of the most meaningful memories I have.

Maybe people change. Maybe life changes. Maybe stories don't always continue exactly the way we imagine them.

But that doesn't make the memories meaningless.

I will always be grateful for the moments that once made me smile.

And if there is one thing I hope you remember, it is simply this:

You were an important chapter of my story. And some chapters deserve to be remembered even after the book is closed.

— Taher
